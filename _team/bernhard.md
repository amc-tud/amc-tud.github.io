**About**  

Bernhard is a professor of Biopsychology at TU Dresden and started the AMC lab in 2022. Before that, he was a research scientist at the Center of Adaptive Rationality (MPIB Berlin), a research fellow at the University of Oxford, and a postdoctoral researcher at FU Berlin and BCCN Berlin (HU/Charité Berlin). He loves cycling and prides himself on never having owned a car.  


**Research Interests:**  

- Working Memory 
- Learning
- Numerical Cognition
- Neural ocillations
- Computational modeling

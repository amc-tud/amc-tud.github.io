Bühlerkolloquium, TU Dresden

April 2026 – July 2026

The Bühlerkolloquium is a talk series organized by the AMC Group at TU Dresden, featuring researchers from leading international institutions presenting their work in cognitive neuroscience.

Talk: Beta frequency shifts in decision making: Spectral fingerprints or communication channels?

Dr. Anastasia Kiyonaga

Talk: Working memory beyond the cortex

Dr. Jakub Limanowski

Universität Greifswald, Germany

Talk: Body models in the brain: Linking adaptive action and self-other distinction

Lab Retreat

February 2026

The AMC Group organized a lab retreat in the beautiful town of Seifhennersdorf.

The retreat included presentations from all lab members, scientific discussions, and social activities including a lab pub quiz.

Conference on Cognitive Neuroscience (CCN) 2025

August 2025 — Amsterdam, The Netherlands

Fabian Kamp, Fabio Bauer, Frieda Born, Felix Bröhl & Ines Pont Sanchis attended the Cognitive Computational Neuroscience Conference (CCN) in Amsterdam.

The conference provided an opportunity for lab members to present their work, exchange ideas, and connect with the international neuroscience community.

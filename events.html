Bühlerkolloquium, TU Dresden

April 2026 – July 2026

The Bühlerkolloquium is a talk series organized by the AMC Group at TU Dresden, featuring researchers from leading international institutions presenting their work on cognition, perception, and brain function.

Dr. Ryszard Auksztulewicz

Institution: Maastricht University, The Netherlands

Talk: Dynamic Predictions: Interactions Between Memory, Context, and Sensory Processing?

Dr. Saskia Haegens

Institution: Columbia University, USA

Talk: Beta frequency shifts in decision making: Spectral fingerprints or communication channels?

Dr. Anastasia Kiyonaga

Institution: University of California, San Diego (UCSD), USA

Talk: Working memory beyond the cortex

Dr. Jakub Limanowski

Institution: Universität Greifswald, Germany

Talk: Body models in the brain: Linking adaptive action and self-other distinction
